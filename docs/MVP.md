
# AI Job Research Assistant — MVP Specification

## 1. Objective

Build an AI-powered assistant that analyzes job opportunities, researches companies, evaluates candidate suitability, and produces actionable research reports.

## 2. Target Users

- Software developers
- Junior engineers
- Interns and graduates
- Freelancers seeking opportunities

## 3. Core User Workflow

1. User submits a job description.
2. System validates the input.
3. AI extracts structured job information.
4. System identifies required skills and qualifications.
5. Research component gathers relevant company information.
6. Matching component compares job requirements against a candidate profile.
7. System identifies strengths and skill gaps.
8. Assistant generates a structured research report.

## 4. MVP Features

### Job Analysis
- Extract job title.
- Identify company name.
- Extract responsibilities.
- Identify required and preferred skills.
- Extract qualifications and experience requirements.

### Company Research
- Gather company information.
- Identify products and services.
- Research company background.
- Provide source references.

### Candidate Matching
- Compare candidate skills against job requirements.
- Identify matching skills.
- Highlight missing skills.
- Provide an explainable suitability assessment.

### Research Report
- Job summary.
- Company overview.
- Requirements breakdown.
- Candidate strengths.
- Skill gaps.
- Recommended next actions.

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements

- Modular architecture.
- Clear error handling.
- Structured AI responses.
- Secure API key management.
- Testable components.
- Source transparency.

## 6. Out of Scope for MVP

- Automatic job applications.
- Autonomous email sending.
- Browser automation.
- Multiple AI agents.
- Full web dashboard.
- Automatic CV submission.

These can be considered in later versions.

## 7. Success Criteria

The MVP should successfully accept a job description and produce a structured, useful research report with clearly identified requirements, company information, candidate matching, and actionable recommendations.

## 8. Development Approach

Build incrementally:

1. Project foundation.
2. Job description input and validation.
3. Job information extraction.
4. Candidate profile management.
5. Skills matching.
6. Company research.
7. Report generation.
8. Testing and integration.
